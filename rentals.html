How our rentals work

Helmet and lock included. Multi-day rentals include a charger.

Standard cruiser rentals

Comfortable, upright electric bikes built for paved paths and city streets – not off-road use. Great for exploring, sightseeing, and locking up to shop, hike, or grab food. Deluxe cruisers add extra conveniences like mirrors and phone holders; standard cruisers keep things simple.

  • Best for paved paths, Garden of the Gods, and city riding
  • Includes helmet, lock, and quick safety orientation
  • Bookable in 2-hour or full-day durations

Deluxe cruiser rentals

Similar upright comfort with added convenience features like mirrors and phone mounts to make navigation and visibility easier while you ride.

  • Comfort upgrades for riders who want extra visibility and navigation help
  • Available in both 2-hour and full-day options
  • Includes the same safety gear and orientation as standard cruisers

Booking & requirements

Book online to view live availability and lock in your preferred time. Please arrive a few minutes early to get fitted, review route ideas, and cover safety basics.

  • Renter must be 21+; riders must be at least 14+
  • Bring a valid ID and payment method
  • Call us with questions or for group bookings
