Mobile Electric Bike Service

Can’t make it to the shop? We’ll come to you.

Our mobile e-bike service brings a qualified mechanic and a full toolkit right to your driveway, garage, or office. Whether you ride for commuting, fitness, or fun, we keep your bike running safely and smoothly without you having to load it into a car.

Service area

  • Colorado Springs
  • Manitou Springs
  • Fountain

If you’re just outside these areas, reach out and we’ll let you know what’s possible.

What we can do on-site

  • New e-bike assembly and safety checks
  • Tune-ups and brake/shift adjustments
  • Flat repair, tire and tube replacement
  • Basic wheel truing and bolt safety check
  • E-bike specific checks, firmware updates (where applicable), and wiring cleanup
  • Installations and upgrades (racks, lights, fenders, accessories)

For larger jobs that require shop equipment, we can help coordinate pickup and return so you still don’t have to move the bike yourself.

How mobile service works

  1. Contact us with your bike details, issues, and location.
  2. We’ll confirm what we can do on-site, give you an estimate, and schedule a time.
  3. On the day of service, we come to you, perform the work, and walk you through what we did and what to expect next.

Onsite at our Manitou Springs shop or mobile at your home or office, our goal is the same – keep you riding more and worrying less.
